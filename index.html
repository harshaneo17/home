About

I'm a machine learning engineer in Newcastle upon Tyne building perception and autonomy for subsea robots (ROVs and AUVs). I develop computer vision and deep learning models, ROS2 drivers for subsea sensors and real-time C++ middleware (RTI DDS), and deploy ML on edge hardware.

Previously I contributed to the world's first commercially available robotic system for post-harvest packing of soft produce at Wootzano. I hold an MSc in Mechatronics with Distinction from Kingston University and have published peer-reviewed research in medical AI. I also write — on technology, and sometimes fiction.
